Ride-sharing app flow

Tap inside the phone to move to the next screen, or use the flow list and the "Next actions" panel to jump to any point. Covers onboarding, search location, available riders, booking, the trip and the driver's offer-a-ride path.

Flow map

Solid arrows show the main path; dashed boxes are alternative or fallback paths. Click any box to open that screen in the demo.

Theme & colour combinations

Brand tokens used across every screen. The same values become the Flutter ThemeData later.

Approved combinations

Use these pairings for text and backgrounds.

White on orange gradientPrimary buttons only. Use bold 15px+ text; contrast is about 3:1, so it is OK for large bold labels but not small text.
Navy on whiteHeadings and key values. High contrast.
Muted grey on whiteSupporting text, hints and captions.
Dark orange on soft orangeChips, badges and selected states.
White on navySelected chips, dark headers and toasts.
Green on soft greenBooked, verified and CO₂-saved messages.
Amber on soft amber"1 seat left" and pending states.
Red on soft redErrors, cancel and SOS.
